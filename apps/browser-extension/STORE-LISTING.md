## Detailed description

Masa Search Inspector shows you, in one click, how a page looks to search engines and to AI answer engines — without leaving the tab.

WHAT IT CHECKS
• SEO — title and meta description (with length), canonical (self vs cross-domain), indexability from meta robots, H1 and heading structure, images missing alt text.
• GEO / AI visibility — whether GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot and more are allowed, blocked or partially blocked by the site's robots.txt.
• Structured data — every JSON-LD type on the page, with invalid blocks flagged.
• Social — Open Graph and Twitter card tags, and hreflang for international pages.
• Links — internal vs external counts and external nofollow.
• Findings — a prioritised, categorised list of what to fix.

It reads the live, rendered DOM, so it reflects JavaScript-rendered content — the version AI crawlers and modern search engines actually see.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing about the pages you visit is sent to us or anyone else. No account, no cookies, no analytics, no background activity. It only acts when you click the icon.
